About the archive

Hi I am Dhruva Myakeri and welcome to the Latent a place for project records, field notes, and slow thinking that doesn’t quite fit into a commit message or a slide deck.

Most of what appears here grows out of work with learning systems, exploring curiosities of "oh what if I do this" "of what if I change this", and the quiet puzzles that show up when theory meets practice. The writing tries to stay close to the observation, and distant from the hype.

You can find all my projects on my github; this site is for the margins- including the full record when something is worth keeping even after it fails cleanly.
